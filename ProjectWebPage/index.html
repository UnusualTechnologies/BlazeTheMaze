BLAZE THE MAZE

A high-speed, real-time multiplayer maze racer for up to 8 players. Every round drops everyone into a fresh procedurally-generated neon labyrinth simultaneously — no turns, no waiting. First Blazer to reach the goal wins.

Power-Ups

Two power-ups are hidden in every maze. Either one can flip the race in an instant:

  • Self-Teleport (white dashed ring) — Instantly warps you to a random cell. Use it to escape a dead end or leapfrog halfway across the grid.
  • Opponent-Teleport (rainbow ring) — Scatters every other player to random cells at once. Grab it while you're winning to blow up the pack behind you.

Multiplayer

  • Instant Match — Hit Race! and you're queued against anyone online worldwide.
  • Friend Code — Generate a private 6-character code and share it. Anyone who enters it joins your room — up to 8 Blazers per match.
  • Goal — Touch the rotating rainbow star first. Your finish time is recorded; try to beat your personal best.
  • Mobile Ready — Full on-screen D-pad for touchscreen play — no keyboard needed.

How to Play

W A S D or Arrow Keys to navigate your Blazer.
On mobile: use the on-screen D-pad.
Don't stop. Don't hit the walls. First to the star wins.
