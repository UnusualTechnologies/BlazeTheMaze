BLAZE THE MAZE

⚡ Race through neon labyrinths. Grab power-ups. Wreck your friends.

> LOADING...

Blaze the Maze is a fast-paced, server-authoritative multiplayer maze racer where speed, precision, and quick reflexes are your only weapons. Up to 8 players race through a fresh procedurally-generated labyrinth every round — simultaneously, no turns. Grab power-ups, outmaneuver your rivals, and reach the goal first.


> POWER-UP MANIFEST

Two power-ups are hidden in every maze. Either one can flip the race in an instant:

  • ⬤ Self-Teleport (spinning white ring) — Instantly warps you to a random cell. Escape a dead end or leapfrog halfway across the grid.
  • ◉ Opponent-Teleport (rainbow ring) — Scatters every other player to random cells at once. Grab it while you're winning to blow up the pack.

> CORE DIRECTIVES

  • ⚡ Real-Time Multiplayer: Built on a Colyseus backend. Every player's position syncs at 60fps — no lag excuses.
  • 🔑 Friend Codes: Generate a private 6-character code and invite friends directly. Up to 8 Blazers per room.
  • 🏁 First to the Star: Touch the rotating rainbow goal first. Your finish time is recorded — race yourself too.
  • 📱 Mobile Ready: Full on-screen D-pad for touchscreen play — no keyboard needed.

> HOW TO PLAY

W A S D or Arrow Keys — navigate your Blazer
Mobile: on-screen D-pad

Don't stop. Don't hit the walls. First to the star wins.
