BLAZE THE MAZE

A high-speed, multiplayer neon maze runner!

> INITIALIZING...

Welcome to the grid. Blaze the Maze is a fast-paced, server-authoritative multiplayer arcade game where speed, precision, and quick reflexes are your only weapons. Navigate the shifting neon mazes, grab power-ups, and outmaneuver your friends to claim victory.


> CORE DIRECTIVES

  • ⚡ Real-Time Multiplayer: Built on a lightning-fast Colyseus backend. Play instantly with anyone around the world.
  • 🎮 Custom Lobbies: Want to settle a score? Generate a 6-character Friend Code and race in private matches with up to 8 players.
  • 🚀 Neon Power-Ups: Collect glowing data-nodes to boost your score and trigger instant teleports across the grid to escape dead ends.
  • 🕹️ Pure Arcade Aesthetic: Glowing graphics, synth-wave visual cues, and a relentless 60fps canvas experience.

HOW TO PLAY

Use W A S D or the Arrow Keys to navigate your Blazer.
Don't hit the walls. Don't stop moving.
